# Caregiver usability protocol — prepared, not performed

No participant sessions or caregiver endorsements have been collected. Automated browser/accessibility checks are not human usability testing. Use synthetic data only and record findings after actual consented sessions.

## Tasks

Ask a caregiver or care coordinator to think aloud without coaching. Use anonymous participant IDs and record role/device familiarity rather than identifying or clinical information.

1. Identify the current concern and the latest observation time. Explain which sources contributed and which observations are old or unavailable.
2. Find the unresolved episode from the overview, acknowledge it, record a simulated action, and resolve with a reason. Explain why acknowledgement and resolution differ.
3. Read a current, a historical/negated, and a failed-analysis note. Explain which can affect the current assessment and where to inspect the source.
4. Disconnect sensor transport, generate three readings, refresh, and reconnect. Find the queue count and distinguish sensor outage from a disconnected dashboard.
5. Leave an alert unanswered, advance the labeled demo clock, switch to the backup identity, and locate the escalation. Explain what in-app escalation or provider acceptance does—and does not—prove.
